### Training

**I train Rite Tec through aerobic forms.** A sport aerobics variant built on patterns and moves, not fixed choreography. It works cardio, rhythm and breath.

**Hand barbells, 1 to 3 kg,** carry the same patterns with load in the hands.

**Body practice** also includes:

- Basic Qigong.
- I Liq Chuan level 1 drills for directions, alignment and the spiral.
- Flexibility drills to rhythm, such as directional popping: hands or legs hit on the beat.

### Mind and body coordination

The mind is always active. It controls the body, reads the moment and listens to the body. When the music comes in, that control becomes expression. The body is the engine that turns music into movement.

I train it with coordination drills:

- **Independent limbs:** each leg and each hand moving separately, placed on purpose.
- **Floor work:** coordination in positions on the floor.
- **Standing work:** the same coordination standing.
- **Directional moves:** body parts coordinated through directions in space.

The aim is precise control of each body part in space. The mind leads, the body answers.

### Here and now: the rite

**The dance floor is a mindfulness practice.** Techno gives a slowly changing, repetitive field. Martial training gives the attention to meet each change as it happens.

- **Techno is built for presence.** Hypnotic techno works through repetition, space and gradual change more than tempo. I cannot plan against it, only notice the next change.
- **The martial arts train that attention.** I Liq Chuan builds on Taiji and Chan: non-assertion, non-resistance, the present moment. The partner is the track. Listen, match, respond, never force.
- **Control of direction.** Every movement resolves onto a known axis: six directions, centerline, diagonals. It arrives on purpose.
- **Control of mass.** I always know where the weight is: empty and full in the legs, the rod as a third point, the root before a release. Controlled mass lets precision survive inside trance.
- **The rite.** Repetition, attention and control turn a club set into a ritual. Lost in the music and fully in charge of the body at once. That paradox is one more form of dissonance.
