The limbs never move on their own. Rite Tec uses physical principles from Chinese internal wushu. Force starts at the floor, is routed by the center and arrives at the hands. Bones work as struts and fascia as cables, so tension carries across the whole body and a hit at the hand can be driven from the foot. Tendons and fascia store energy and release it like elastic bands, which is why I prefer springy, repeated movement over grinding effort.

### Main principles

| Principle | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Power from the ground | Every hit starts with a press of the foot into the floor. Nothing is done by the arm alone. |
| The spiral | Force turns through the waist and spine on its way up. Transitions are spirals, not straight lines. |
| Load and release | Tension builds in the spiral and lets go at the hand. Staying soft between hits makes the hits read as hard. |
| Empty and full | One leg carries the weight at a time. Every step has a clear base, which keeps fast footwork clean. |
| Centerlines | The body works along more than one line. Bones stack along each line toward its aim, so stops look locked, not tense. |

### Space

Straight lines for the signal, spirals for the transmission, verticals for the engine.

| Idea | In Rite Tec |
| --- | --- |
| Six directions | Forward and back, left and right, up and down. Every stop and plane resolves onto one of these axes. |
| Straight and diagonal | Robot, mime and popping live on straight lines through the center. Spirals and crossing hand work travel the diagonals. Switching between them is a core tool. |
| Facing | The main centerline is the body's aim. Turns rotate it in clear steps, so dancing in the round stays readable from any side. |

### Ranges

| Range | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Long | Empty-handed, the body fully stretched. Phrases often compress to near reach, then snap out to long range on the drop. |
| Medium | The range of control and strength. The hand releases fully, or locks instead, which leans towards robot and creates distortion, dissonance and illusions that trick the eye. |
| Short | Also with the rod. The most ritual range: it turns inward and closes the body, following its natural form. |
